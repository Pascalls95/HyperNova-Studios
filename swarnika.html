CYBERDASH

Swipe, Collect and Move onto the Higher levels!

Synopsis

CyberDash is an exciting educational game that teaches students important cybersafety skills while they play. Players must navigate through different levels, collecting lives and safe keys to earn points while avoiding dangerous phish keys. If a player accidentally collects a phish key, they lose a life! At the end of each round, players must answer a cybersafety question by selecting the correct answer. There are four challenging levels, with each level helping players learn more about how to stay safe online.

How to Play

  • Move your character around the game using the arrow keys.
  • Collect safe keys to earn points.
  • Collect lives to increase your chances of completing the level.
  • Avoid phish keys, as they will make you lose a life.
  • Reach the end of each round to receive a cybersafety question.
  • Choose the correct answer to the question to continue.
  • Complete all four levels and try to achieve the highest score possible!

Aim

The aim of CyberDash is to make learning about cybersafety fun and interactive. The game teaches students how to recognise online dangers, make safe decisions and understand basic cybersafety concepts while challenging them to achieve a high score.

Controls

  • Up arrow - Move up
  • Down arrow - Move down
  • Left arrow - Move left
  • Right arrow - Move right

Platform Requirements

CyberDash is designed to be played on a computer or laptop with a keyboard.
