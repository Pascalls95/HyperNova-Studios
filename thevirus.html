The VIRUS.exe

Fight corrupted enemies in strategic, turn-based battles and collect powerups to defeat THE VIRUS!

Synopsis

The VIRUS.exe is a pixel art RPG where a single click of a suspicious link unleashes a dangerous digital virus into the real world. Explore the town with the mysterious Bit Converter, collect cybersecurity upgrades and battle corrupted enemies in turn based combat. Answer cybersecurity questions correctly to activate and power your attacks and stop THE VIRUS before it spreads and takes control over everything…

How to Play

Aim

Collect different powerups around the town to unlock new moves. Use these moves to defeat enemies in turn-based combat, unlocking each ability by answering cybersecurity questions as you progress through the map and collect new powerups! After collecting all the powerups, use your attacks to defeat the final enemy: THE VIRUS!

Controls

This game requires a keyboard and mouse to play.
Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move around
Use E to open/close your inventory
Click on unlocked attacks in battle to use them

Platform Requirements

Operating System:

Windows , macOS , Linux , ChromeOS

Browser:

Chrome, Safari, Microsoft Edge, FireFox, Opera, Brave or any other modern web browser.
